### F2: A retained inactive revision requires manual reactivation (skipped)

- **severity:** Warning
- **status:** skipped

- **concern:** After a manual rollback, deploying the retained newer revision again fails at mkdir because its release directory already exists.

### F3: Late verification failure does not roll back successful activation (skipped)

- **severity:** Warning
- **status:** skipped
- **location:** ops/deploy-release.sh:43-54
- **concern:** If a service or health check fails after activation succeeds, deployment reports failure but leaves the new release active.
- **resolution:** Skip; activation already checks loopback health and the worker and rolls back startup failures. A later failure needs health to change in the short interval or an independent shared-service event. Systemd retries process failures, but persistent failures can require manual rollback. A second rollback transaction adds restart and recovery failure paths, can revert healthy releases for transient checks, and cannot repair other applications or undo migrations; that cost outweighs the residual risk for this operator-run command.
