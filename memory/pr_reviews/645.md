## Findings

### F8: Phrases match across an exclamation mark (fixed)

- **severity:** Warning
- **status:** fixed
- **location:** src/server/word-filter.ts:56
- **concern:** "!" stayed inside words as a look-alike, so a sentence ending in "!" still joined a phrase, and ordinary text such as "Lend a hand! Job well done." or "This show is batty! Boy, did I laugh." was refused, against the F1 rule and the docs.
- **resolution:** An exclamation mark before a space now ends a phrase, while the word before it still reads with the mark as a look-alike; matcher cases pin it, red first, and the docs describe it.
- **fixed_at:** 2026-11-10

### F6: Digits around a phrase in a username hide it (fixed)

- **severity:** Warning
- **status:** fixed
- **location:** src/server/word-filter.ts:16
- **concern:** Phrase words were read only with look-alike symbols trimmed, not digits, so usernames such as "blow_job69", "gang_bang99", and "69blow_job" passed while "blowjob69" and "nigger99" were refused, against the documented rule that digits around a word don't join it.
- **resolution:** Each word of a phrase now reads every way a single word does, digits around it included; matcher cases and the every-entry test's username form pin it, red first.
- **fixed_at:** 2026-11-10

### F7: A phrase matches after a compound's last word (skipped)

- **severity:** Warning
- **status:** skipped
- **location:** src/server/word-filter.ts:16
- **concern:** Phrase matching ignores the word before a phrase, so ordinary text such as "He quit his farm hand job" or "a second-hand job" is refused as "hand job".
- **resolution:** Skip: such phrasing is rare, the member keeps the text and can reword, and telling the compound apart needs a list of preceding words to maintain, while "hand job" is the term's usual spelling.

### F4: Spaced spellings of listed explicit terms pass (fixed)

- **severity:** Warning
- **status:** fixed
- **location:** src/server/blocked-words.ts
- **concern:** "gangbang", "rimjob", "footjob", "cumshot", "cumslut", and "titfuck" were listed as joined words, so their dictionary spellings "gang bang", "rim job", "cum shot", and hyphenated forms such as "gang-bang" passed, while "blow job" and "hand job" matched every spelling.
- **resolution:** The six are phrases, which match spaced, hyphenated, and joined; the list comment and docs say to list a spaced term as a phrase unless its spaced form is ordinary text, as with "creampie" and "deepthroat". The every-entry test checks all three spellings.
