### F1: Removing a review from the reader's own review entry drops focus (fixed)

- **severity:** Warning
- **status:** fixed
- **location:** src/components/review.tsx:267, src/components/entry-title-actions.tsx:83
- **concern:** Remove review on the reader's own review entry without replies takes the entry and its button out of the feed, so focus fell to the page body instead of the nearest entry, as the PR's other own-entry removals do.
- **resolution:** `useReviewEditor` takes `leaving` focus targets and wraps Remove in removal focus; feed entries pass `entryFocus` for the reader's own entries. `feed-entry-actions.spec.ts` failed before and passes after.
- **fixed_at:** 2026-11-10

### F2: Removal focus plans linger on entries whose control stays (skipped)

- **severity:** Warning
- **status:** skipped
- **location:** src/components/entry-title-actions.tsx:110
- **concern:** Every Recommend or status change starts a removal focus plan, and on a friend's entry the control stays, so the plan waits until that entry leaves later and then moves focus, scrolling, to a neighbor if focus is on the page body.
