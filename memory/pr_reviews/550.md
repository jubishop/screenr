## Findings

### F1: Single-entry lookups reach the person's other card  (fixed)

- **severity:** Critical
- **status:** fixed
- **location:** src/server/feed.ts (itemQuery)
- **concern:** itemQuery("c.id=$2") also returned the holder of any other card the same person had for the title, and thread, addComment, and setReaction took rows[0], so a reply or reaction to a lone entry or standalone comment could land on that card.
- **resolution:** The holder clause now admits only holders of cards with an entry in scope. A new feed-cards test covering a lone status, a standalone comment, and a separate card failed first and passes.
- **fixed_at:** 2026-11-08

### F2: Notification excerpt ignores a card's spoiler review  (fixed)

- **severity:** Warning
- **status:** fixed
- **location:** src/server/notifications.ts (notification)
- **concern:** A reply on a status or Recommend entry holding a card with a spoiler-flagged review sent its excerpt to the push extension, though the feed covers the card.
- **resolution:** The single-notification read also checks the card's spoiler flag through the feed's item read for status and Recommend entries; docs updated. A notifications test failed first and passes.
- **fixed_at:** 2026-11-08

### F3: Want to watch saves from a card lose the Recommend surface  (fixed)

- **severity:** Warning
- **status:** fixed
- **location:** src/components/feed.tsx, ios/ScreenrKit/Sources/ScreenrKit/Conversation/FeedModel.swift
- **concern:** Web and ScreenrKit chose friend_recommendation only when the holder was the Recommend, so saves from the common review-held card counted as friend_entry and undercounted the observed save measure.
- **resolution:** Both clients use the card-level recommended flag. A browser step and a ScreenrKit test failed first and pass; docs/usage-telemetry.md updated.
- **fixed_at:** 2026-11-08

### F4: Card split counts as New activity  (skipped)

- **severity:** Nit
- **status:** skipped
- **location:** src/feed-layout.ts, ios/ScreenrKit/Sources/ScreenrKit/Conversation/FeedLayout.swift
- **concern:** When a friend's review edited over 24 hours later leaves a card, the remaining entries' card has an older activity time than the accepted one, so it counts once behind New activity with nothing new.
- **resolution:** Skipped: a rare, self-clearing extra count with no lost content does not justify new layout state in both clients and the shared cases.
