# Product brief

Screenr is a social app for TV and movies, similar in concept to Goodreads.

## Confirmed requirements

- Users can have friends.
- Users can see what their friends are watching or recommend.
- Users can make their own recommendations.
- Users can choose whether each recommendation is globally visible.
- Users can choose to explore global recommendations.

These requirements came from the initial product discussion on 2026-09-04.
They do not yet define the detailed permission model or first-release scope.

## Open decisions

- The main reason to open the app and the first audience to serve.
- Launch platform and application stack.
- Friend relationships, requests, following, and discovery.
- Watch statuses, watch history, and TV season or episode tracking.
- Recommendation format, ratings, and spoiler handling.
- Visibility defaults, the audience for non-public recommendations, and
  what happens when visibility or a friendship changes.
- Global discovery, ranking, reporting, and moderation.
- Movie and TV metadata source, authentication, and hosting.
- The smallest useful first release and how to judge its success.

## First question to resolve

What should be the main reason someone opens Screenr?

The proposed starting point is: “Help me find something to watch, based on
people I trust.” This is a recommendation for discussion, not an accepted
decision.
